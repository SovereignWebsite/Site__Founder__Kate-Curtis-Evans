                                   Summary
                                   I have spent 6 years transcribing for legal professionals across
                                   the UK, I still do the work every day, and that’s exactly why I built
                                   Audio Genius. I kept seeing the same problem which is that
                                   most transcription technology is built for organisations processing
                                   audio, or for people capturing their own meetings. Very little of
                                   it is designed around the person actually producing the finished
                                   transcript, the professional working from someone else’s audio,
                                   dealing with multiple speakers, difficult recordings, exacting
                                   standards, confidentiality requirements and deadline pressure.I built
                                   what I wanted to use myself. I had no outside funding, and definitely
                                   no traditional tech background. I started with six years of domain
                                   expertise, a very clear idea of what was missing, and the conviction
                                   that advances in speech recognition could make professional
                                   transcription significantly more efficient without removing the
                                   professional from the process.And so Audio Genius was born, an
                                   ASR-powered legal transcription and workflow platform designed
                                   around human-led transcription. The technology does the heavy
                                   lifting, but the professional retains control. And because legal audio
                                   can contain highly sensitive information, security, confidentiality and
                                   responsible data handling aren't features to bolt on later, they're
                                   fundamental to what we're building.I'm now developing Audio
                                   Genius with feedback from real transcription professionals. Some
                                   of the best ideas for the platform are already coming directly from
                                   the people testing it, and that is exactly how the product should
                                   evolve. I'm not a tech founder guessing at what professional
                                   transcribers need. I am one, and that's the difference.I'm currently
                                   looking to connect with:→ Law firms and transcription agencies
                                   exploring secure transcription and workflow technology for their in-
                                   house teams and freelancer networks→ Professional transcribers
                                   and legal professionals who want to help shape the technology
                                   they'll actually use→ Investors interested in vertical SaaS built from
