Five designs for Kate Curtis‑Evans

Each is an idea from Kate's own world that rebuilds the page. Every word on them is from her own LinkedIn profile and posts, as on the live page.

  1. 1. The Workflow Board (the home page)

    Audio Genius manages transcription work, so Kate's own work is a board: delivered, in progress, on hold on purpose, and one open job, the technical co-founder.

  2. 2. The Transcript

    The page as a legal transcript, the document Kate produces every day: a cover sheet, numbered turns, Q and A, her own words verbatim, the beta figures as Exhibit A.

  3. 3. The Edit Window

    Designed dark first, as a transcription editor: a waveform, her roles as tracks on a timeline drawn to the year, each section a segment with its label.

  4. 4. The Newsroom

    Her founder story told as a news feature: the specialist who became a founder. A headline, a standfirst, the lead photo, the story with her quote pulled out, and the figures in an at-a-glance box.

  5. 5. The Brief

    Plain and printable for people in a hurry: the ask, the product, the figures and her record on one or two sheets of A4.

Earlier designs, kept so old links work: Foundations and The Profile (the first draft).
