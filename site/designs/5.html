Kate Curtis‑Evans

Founder, Audio Genius. Based in the United Kingdom.

A practising legal transcriber who built a live transcription platform without knowing how to code. Now looking for a technical co-founder.

  • LinkedIn: linkedin.com/in/kate-curtis-evans
  • audio-genius.com

Wanted: a technical co-founder

To own the technical scope, the build, integration and implementation: moving to a tech stack built for regulated data and getting certifications like Cyber Essentials in place. Ideally someone with legal tech or regulated-industry experience.

Kate brings the vision for the platform, direct access to users, research and results, and a strategy for expanding into corporates and organisations.

Why now: the platform Kate built Audio Genius on will not let her fork a test site from the live one, and gives her limited control over hosting and other parts of the build. She wants to own how it scales.

Audio Genius

An ASR-powered legal transcription and workflow platform designed around human-led transcription. The technology does the heavy lifting, but the professional keeps control. Security, confidentiality and responsible data handling are fundamental, not features to bolt on later. As of November 2026, Kate has stopped adding new features on purpose so that privacy and data protection come first.
