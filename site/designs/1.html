Kate Curtis‑Evans

A practising legal transcriber who built a live transcription platform without knowing how to code.

Role
Founder, Audio Genius
Based
United Kingdom
Matter
Now looking for a technical co-founder
Message Kate on LinkedInVisit audio-genius.com

Set out as a legal transcript, the work Kate does every day. The questions are the site's own; every answer is from Kate's own profile and posts.

  1. 00:00:04Q.

    Who built Audio Genius?

  2. 00:00:24A.

    Kate has spent six years transcribing for legal professionals across the UK, and she still does the work every day.

  3. 00:00:47Q.

    What problem did she keep seeing?

  4. 00:01:13A.

    Most transcription technology is built for organisations processing audio, or for people capturing their own meetings. Very little of it is designed around the person producing the finished transcript from someone else's audio, with multiple speakers, difficult recordings, exacting standards, confidentiality requirements and deadline pressure.

  5. 00:01:42Q.

    How did she build it?

  6. 00:01:51A.

    She built what she wanted to use herself, with no outside funding and no traditional tech background. She taught herself to build with vibe coding and AI tools, and learnt to test properly before anything goes live.

  7. 00:02:03Kate Curtis-Evans

    “I'm not a tech founder guessing at what professional transcribers need. I am one, and that's the difference.”Kate, on her LinkedIn profile

  8. 00:02:18Q.

    What is Audio Genius?

  9. 00:02:36A.

    An ASR-powered legal transcription and workflow platform designed around human-led transcription. The technology does the heavy lifting, but the professional keeps control. Because legal audio can hold highly sensitive information, Kate treats security, confidentiality and responsible data handling as fundamental, not features to bolt on later.

    It is being developed with feedback from real transcription professionals, and some of the best ideas for the platform have come directly from the people testing it.

  10. 00:02:57Q.

    Where does it stand?

  11. 00:03:21A.
    Exhibit A: in Kate's words
    50testers in a 90-day beta
    34,000minutes of audio transcribed since launch in September
    1,200website visits, from Google, social media and elsewhere, with no paid advertising as yet

    It now has paying users, and not one of those who signed up at the end of the beta has left. They use it every day. Audio Genius is bootstrapped and pre-seed.

    Figures exactly as Kate stated them in her LinkedIn post looking for a technical co-founder, as given to this site on 20 November 2026.

  12. 00:03:48Q.

    Why has she stopped adding features?

  13. 00:03:55A.

    On purpose, including a legal glossary and template uploads she was looking forward to building. What comes first is the part nobody sees: privacy and data protection, working through what the platform holds and how long it keeps it, one decision at a time.

  14. 00:04:05Q.

    Who is she looking for?

  15. 00:04:18A.

    A technical co-founder. The platform Kate built Audio Genius on will not let her fork a test site from the live one, and gives her limited control over hosting and other parts of the build. She wants to own how it scales. The next stage is moving to a tech stack built for regulated data and getting certifications like Cyber Essentials in place. That is the work she wants a partner to own, ideally someone with legal tech or regulated-industry experience.

    What Kate bringsThe vision for the platform, direct access to users, research and results, and a strategy for expanding into corporates and organisations.
    What the co-founder would ownThe technical scope, the build, integration and implementation.

    If that is you, message her. If you know who it is, send her an introduction.

  16. 00:04:34Q.

    Who else should get in touch?

  17. 00:04:53A.
    • Law firms, agenciesExploring secure transcription and workflow technology for in-house teams and freelancer networks.
    • TranscribersAnd legal professionals who want to help shape the technology they will actually use.
    • InvestorsInterested in vertical SaaS built from genuine domain expertise and a clearly identified industry problem.
    • PartnersLegalTech, speech technology and security partners who understand the technical and compliance challenges of handling professional and legal audio.
  18. 00:05:15Q.

    What is her record?

  19. 00:05:40A.
    • Nov 2025 to nowFounder, Audio Genius. A secure transcription workspace designed to support professional transcription workflows.
    • Mar 2021 to nowTranscription Specialist (Legal and Professional). Secure, human-led transcription for legal and professional clients, with confidential handling: no AI tools used for sensitive material.
    • 2022 to nowLegal Transcriber, eScribers. Professional legal transcription focused on accuracy, consistency and careful handling of sensitive material.
    • 2021 to 2022Freelance Transcriber, Ubiqus.
    • 1992 to 1996BA in European Business Studies, Brunel University of London.
