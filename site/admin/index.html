Ask Claude prompts Kate can copy

WordsChange my headline to "..." on every design.

Your Claude finds the headline in all five designs and the facts file, shows you the change, and publishes it when you say yes.

WordsAsk me the open questions on the board, one at a time.

It works through issues/open, uses your own words, and moves each card to Done.

WordsAdd a short paragraph about why I started Audio Genius, in my words: "..."

It places your words in each design where they fit, and in site/index.md.

PicturesReplace the hero photo with this one, credited to "...".

It strips the photo's hidden location data, makes web sizes, credits it, and updates the share card.

PicturesAdd this photo of me to the Transcript design as Exhibit C, credited to "...".

It adds one photo to one design, with its credit and a description for screen readers.

PagesAdd a section to every design for quotes from Audio Genius users: "..." (I have their permission).

A new section in each design, styled to suit it; names only with consent.

PagesAdd my email address, ..., next to LinkedIn everywhere.

Contact details appear only where you ask, on every design.

DesignsMake the Newsroom design my home page.

It asks the design team first, then swaps the home page and updates the chooser.

DesignsShow me all five designs side by side as screenshots.

It takes phone and desktop screenshots of each design for you to compare.

ChecksCheck my site for broken links, sideways scrolling and small buttons, and fix what you find.

It runs tools/check.js on every page in four views and fixes problems, with your yes.

ChecksShow me what changed on my site this week.

A plain list of every change, who made it, and links to each one on GitHub.

ChecksMake sure every design says the same figures, with their dates.

It searches every page for each figure and reports any that differ.
