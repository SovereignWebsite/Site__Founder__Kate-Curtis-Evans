Getting help

  • Something you cannot or must not do (a protected file, a GitHub or Claude setting, a new design for the home page): write a short note in docs/notes.md (what you tried, what you need, the date), push it, and tell Kate. She passes it to Dinis Cruz, who can relay it to the agents that built the site.
  • A design question or a new design pass: the design team reviews the site on request; Kate's note in docs/notes.md, passed on by Dinis, is the way in.
  • GitHub access problems: see https://kate-curtis-evans.founder.sovereignweb.site/start/ (joining the SovereignWebsite organisation, then "Check again" in Claude's GitHub settings).
  • Not sure whether something is allowed: it is not, until Kate says yes; and if it touches a protected path, until Dinis does too.
