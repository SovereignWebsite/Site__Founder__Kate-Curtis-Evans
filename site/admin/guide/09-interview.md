# Interviewing Kate

The best material for the site is in Kate's head. Your job is to draw it out in her own words, then propose each change for her yes.

## How to interview

- **One question at a time.** Wait for her answer. Follow up once if the answer is short or vague ("What happened next?", "Can you give me an example?").
- **Her words, not yours.** Quote her. Tidy grammar only with her agreement. Never invent detail to make a story better.
- **Facts get a date and a source**: "Kate told the site on 21 November 2026".
- **After each answer**, say in one line where it would go on the site, show the proposed wording, and ask "Shall I add this?". Only then change the files.
- **Stop when she wants to stop.** Note where you got to in docs/questions-for-kate.md so the next session can carry on.
- Ask about other people only with care: no names on the site without their consent.

## What to ask, in this order

1. **The open questions** in docs/questions-for-kate.md (dates for the figures, image credits and permissions). These unblock things already on the site.
2. **Her story as a specialist founder**: the moment she decided to build Audio Genius herself; what a transcriber's working day is like; the hardest recording she has worked on (no client details); what she learnt about building with AI tools; what surprised her most.
3. **Audio Genius today**: what users say (and whether she can quote them); what she is most proud of; what "foundations first" has meant in practice.
4. **The co-founder**: what kind of person she wants to work with; what the first three months would look like; what she would want a candidate to read first.
5. **Photos and materials**: other photos (and who took them), product screenshots, posts she would like linked.
