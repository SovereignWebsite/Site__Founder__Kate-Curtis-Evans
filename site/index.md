# Kate Curtis-Evans

Founder of Audio Genius (https://audio-genius.com/), legal transcription and workflow management, built by a transcriber, for transcription professionals. Based in the United Kingdom. Looking for a technical co-founder.

Every fact here comes from Kate's own LinkedIn profile and posts, given to this site on 20 November 2026.

## Her story
- Kate has spent six years transcribing for legal professionals across the UK, and still does the work every day.
- Most transcription technology is built for organisations processing audio, or for people capturing their own meetings. Very little is designed around the person producing the finished transcript from someone else's audio, with multiple speakers, difficult recordings, exacting standards, confidentiality requirements and deadline pressure.
- She built what she wanted to use herself, with no outside funding and no traditional tech background. She taught herself to build with vibe coding and AI tools, and learnt to test properly before anything goes live.
- In her words: "I'm not a tech founder guessing at what professional transcribers need. I am one, and that's the difference."

## Audio Genius
- An ASR-powered legal transcription and workflow platform designed around human-led transcription. The technology does the heavy lifting, but the professional keeps control.
- Security, confidentiality and responsible data handling are fundamental, not features to bolt on later.
- Foundations first: Kate has stopped adding new features on purpose (including a legal glossary and template uploads) to focus on privacy and data protection.
- Figures, as Kate gave them on 20 November 2026: a 90-day beta with 50 testers; 46,700 minutes of audio transcribed since launch in September 2026; 1,552 website visits in the 90 days to 20 November 2026, with no paid advertising. It now has paying users, and not one of those who signed up at the end of the beta has left. Bootstrapped and pre-seed.

## Wanted: a technical co-founder
- The next stage: moving to a tech stack built for regulated data and getting certifications like Cyber Essentials in place. Ideally someone with legal tech or regulated-industry experience.
- Kate brings: the vision for the platform, direct access to users, research and results, and a strategy for expanding into corporates and organisations.
- The co-founder would own: the technical scope, the build, integration and implementation.
