Start here: taking over your website

Your site is live at kate-curtis-evans.founder.sovereignweb.site and it is yours. These steps take about ten minutes, once. After that you change the site by asking your own Claude. Steps as of 20 November 2026.

  1. Accept the GitHub invitation

    GitHub has invited your GitHub account to work on the repository that holds your site. Sign in to GitHub with that account, then open:

    Open the invitation

    Click Accept invitation. That is all.

    If that page says "404" or "not found": you are not signed in, or you are signed in with a different GitHub account. Sign in at github.com/login with the account the invitation was sent to, then click the button above again.

    Other ways in: the email from GitHub that says you were invited to collaborate on SovereignWebsite/Site__Founder__Kate-Curtis-Evans has a "View invitation" button; or, signed in, open the repository and accept from the banner at the top.

    If it says the invitation has expired (GitHub invitations last seven days), ask Dinis to send it again.

    You will know it worked when the repository opens for you without a banner, and you are listed with access.

  2. Connect your Claude to GitHub

    In the browser or the Claude app (needs a paid Claude plan, Pro or above):

    1. Go to claude.ai/code and sign in.
    2. Follow the prompt to connect GitHub, and approve it on GitHub's page. To do this again later: claude.ai/connect-github.
    3. Click the repository selector below the message box and choose SovereignWebsite/Site__Founder__Kate-Curtis-Evans, with the branch set to dev.

    Or on your own computer: install Claude Code (Mac: curl -fsSL https://claude.ai/install.sh | bash; Windows PowerShell: irm https://claude.ai/install.ps1 | iex), run claude and sign in, then sign in to GitHub with GitHub's own tool from cli.github.com by running gh auth login.

    You never need to copy or paste a token or password: every sign-in is GitHub's or Claude's own page. If the repository does not appear in the list, tell Dinis.

    Sources: Claude Code on the web, quickstart and Claude Code setup.

  3. Paste this into your Claude

    Tap inside the box, select all, copy, and paste it as your first message. It tells your Claude what the site is, its rules and how to work.

    The same text is in the repository as docs/prompt-for-your-claude.md.

  4. Make your first change

    A good first request: "Ask me the questions in docs/questions-for-kate.md, one at a time, and update the site from my answers." They cover the dates for your figures, who took your headshot, which images may be used, and your story as a specialist who became a founder.

    Your Claude shows you the change, and when you say yes it pushes it. It is live a minute or two later; you can watch it publish in the repository's Actions tab.

  5. Choose your design

    Your site comes in five designs of the same facts; the "Choose a design" bar at the top of every page switches between them. See all five, then tell your Claude which one you want as the home page, or keep them all.
